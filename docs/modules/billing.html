The New Invoice catalogues are capped at 500 rows — 1,079 KB, and the rest is invisible BI8 · high

Choosing a department on the New Invoice tab pulls that catalogue with limit=500: /laboratory?resource=tests 287.3 KB / 500 rows, /radiology?resource=exams 196.8 KB / 500 rows, /pharmacy/drugs 595.0 KB / 500 rows — 1,079 KB across the three. The pharmacy catalogue is roughly 200,000 rows, so a cashier can reach 0.25% of it and the other 99.75% reports as "no results" for drugs that exist. This is rule 5: a cap is not pagination, and rows past it vanish with no message.

One correction to how this was first described: the three do not load together when the tab opens. Each fires only when its department is selected, so a single bill costs one of the three, not all 1,079 KB. The cap is the defect; the bundling was not real.

Every keystroke, filter and clear fires four requests where one is needed BI9 · high

Three actions were measured and all three behave the same way. Typing INV into the invoice search fired four requests and only one carried the search term; the other three — ?resource=services, ?resource=stats and /insurance — re-read data no user input had touched. Clearing the search: four again. Changing the status filter: four again.

The cause is one effect at BillingModule.jsx:622-629. fetchAll bundles four fetchers, and fetchBills closes over page, search, status, type and date — so its identity changes on every interaction, which changes fetchAll, which re-runs the effect, which calls all four. The other three depend on none of it.

This is the measured, per-request form of the vaguer "queries refetch when nothing has changed" already recorded against this module.

measured 10 August, e2e/verify-billing-claims.mjs — search "INV": 4 requests, 1 carried search=; clear: 4 requests, 1 useful; status filter: 4 requests, 1 carried status=paid

Fixed3 items

Verified after the change, not just edited.

There was no way to ask what was billed today BI5 · fixed

Invoices could be narrowed by search, status and type — never by date. The one question a counter asks every evening had no control at all, and the backend did not read startDate/endDate either.

It now reuses useDateFilter, the control Patients, Pre-Triage and Queue already share, and filters in the DATABASE: a page-local filter would only ever search the ten rows on screen and would report "no invoices in March" while March sat on page four.

Two details that decide whether it is right: dayRange resolves the day in the hospital's timezone, so a bill raised after 18:30 IST does not fall into tomorrow; and an empty range is not assigned at all, because invoiceDate: {} matches nothing in Prisma and a blank filter would have returned zero invoices instead of all.

backend/scripts/billing-filter-matrix.mjs — all 16 filter combinations, 0 problems, plus four date checks including that an invoice is found on its IST day

Two Clear buttons a step apart, the left one doing less BI6 · fixed

The shared date control renders its own Clear, and this tab needs one that also resets search, status and type. Both rendered showed "Clear" and "Clear all" side by side — press the wrong one and the list looks unfiltered while the search box is still narrowing it.

useDateFilter now takes showClear, defaulting to true. Patients, Pre-Triage and Queue are untouched: a date filter is all they have, so its own button is their only way to reset it. Billing passes false and offers one "Clear all".

e2e/verify-billing-datefilter.mjs — 9/9, including that Clear all strips every filter from the request and then hides itself
